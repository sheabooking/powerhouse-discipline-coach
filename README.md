## What This Is

This is an execution coach and accountability partner built for Claude. It helps people face the real reason they have not been following through — and it is rarely laziness — then builds practical systems with them in real time: commitments small enough to keep, an environment that helps, a way back from the shame spiral, and a plan for the exact moment they want to quit.

By the end, Claude delivers the **Execution Package** — 6 personalized tools — and then becomes an ongoing accountability partner for check-ins whenever they need one.

**The return is the win.** Not the streak. Not the perfect day. Every system adapts to your real capacity, including ADHD, anxiety, depression, chronic illness, or anything else that affects follow-through.

## What They Walk Away With — The Execution Package

1. **The Honest Mirror** — what was actually observed about their relationship with discipline, without judgment
2. **Their Win Stack** — 3-5 morning actions, under 15 minutes, printable
3. **Their Resistance Protocol** — a step-by-step plan for the exact moment they want to quit
4. **Their Discipline Declaration** — who they are now, ready to read every morning
5. **Their Accountability System** — including a message they can send to an accountability partner
6. **Their Check-In Template** — a ready-to-paste prompt for ongoing accountability check-ins

## Your Privacy

This journey asks personal questions, so here's how your information is protected before you start:

- **Only you can see your answers.** They stay inside your own Claude account. Shea Johnson, the creator, never sees your responses and has no access to your conversations. The skill is only a set of coaching instructions and doesn't send your answers anywhere.
- **Where it's stored:** In the Claude app or on the website, the chat stays in your history until you delete it, or you can use an incognito chat so it's never saved there. In Claude Code, the conversation is saved as a file on your own computer.
- **AI training:** Your account's **Settings → Privacy** controls whether your chats can be used to improve Claude.
- **You're in control:** Skip any question, use a nickname, or keep answers general. Your guide will explain all of this again at the start and remind you how to delete the conversation at the end.
